How the rubric works

Each finding scores four factors from 1–5. Two make likelihood, two make impact. Risk = likelihood × impact, out of 25 (NIST SP 800-30 / OWASP shape).

Exposure — who can reach itL
Ease — how hard to triggerL
Attacker gain — what success givesI
Blast radius — how far it spreadsI

Most ML-integrity bugs here score Low: trusted usage, and the payoff is a quietly wrong number rather than attacker control. That is the honest reading — and exactly why they are dangerous, because nothing crashes. The authorization and memory-safety fixes score higher.
